There is no one who can open it for you

You choose the Google account, the devices, and the people a keyring is shared with. You keep the recovery code. Keyweb has no server that can reset a lock, replace a lost recovery code, or open a vault on your behalf.

If the recovery code is lost, and so is every device that can unlock the vault, the vault cannot be opened. That follows from the encryption. It is not a failure that can be undone afterwards.

What you take on

  • Keep the recovery code somewhere you can find it, and not only inside Keyweb. Anyone who has the code and the Drive file can open the vault.
  • Keep your devices and your Google account under your control. Keyweb cannot protect a device or a Google account that someone else can already use.
  • Share a keyring only with people you mean to share it with, and read the sharing key aloud when Keyweb asks you to. A link sent over chat can be swapped on the way.
  • Do not put a secret into Keyweb unless you are willing to be the one who keeps it. If the secret belongs to someone else, you need the right to store it and to share it.
